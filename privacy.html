Your information

Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated November 11, 2026.

This notice describes the current local version of Everyday Find Studio – Affiliate Studio and the informational website for Everyday Find Studio. The app is operated by the brand's owner in Canada for their own content workflow. It does not offer public accounts or connect other people's social accounts.

Information in the local app

The owner enters product names and descriptions, product and affiliate links, image references, image-permission notes, creative briefs, captions, review decisions and schedules. Imported affiliate reports can include clicks, conversions and reported commissions. This information is used to prepare, review, plan and evaluate the owner's content.

The app stores workspace records in a local SQLite database and images in local files. Exports and backups are separate files under the owner's control. Platform credentials are configured locally in environment settings. The app does not ask visitors for Pinterest passwords or session cookies.

If you email us, we receive your email address, name if supplied, and message content. We use that correspondence to answer your request and address follow-up questions. Our mailbox is provided by Gmail, so email is also processed by Google under its privacy policy. Please include only information needed for your request.

Platform connections and sharing

Pinterest API access is not yet connected. If authorized and enabled, an explicit publication sends Pinterest the selected image, title, caption, destination link and board identifier using the account's access token. The app records the resulting post identifier, URL and publication status locally. It does not read private messages, scrape other accounts, or perform automatic likes, follows or comments.

If Instagram publication is separately configured, the selected caption and a public image URL are sent to Meta. Local image generation sends the selected reference and brief to the configured ComfyUI server. Remote reference-image downloads also contact the image host. Information shared with these services is subject to their own policies and the owner's configuration.

We do not sell information collected through this app. We do not use Pinterest data to train AI models. The owner reviews each creative and explicitly initiates live publication; reaching a scheduled time alone does not publish a post.

Website visits and external links

These informational pages contain no analytics scripts, contact forms, tracking pixels or application cookies added by us. Website hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs to deliver and secure the site, including on infrastructure outside Canada. We do not operate a visitor-tracking database or set GitHub's infrastructure-log retention periods. GitHub describes its own data practices in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Following a Pinterest, retailer or affiliate link takes you to another service. That service may collect information or use cookies under its own policies. Affiliate links can enable the retailer or affiliate network to attribute a purchase. This website does not receive payment-card details or process orders.

Retention, access and deletion

The owner controls local records, media, credentials, exports and backups and should retain them only while needed for the stated purposes. Local product deletion removes associated database records, creatives and schedules; media files, exported packages and backups require separate removal. Local deletion does not remove an existing Pinterest or Instagram post.

Privacy-request correspondence is kept while needed to respond, follow up and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. You can ask the owner to review or delete correspondence using the contact below.

When a platform connection ends or an applicable deletion request is received, the owner must review and remove the relevant local platform information and copies, subject to applicable requirements. No automatic retention purge is implemented. Access can also be revoked through the relevant platform's app settings. Questions about access, correction or deletion should be sent to the contact below.

Security and changes

The content app is intended to run on the owner's computer using localhost. The informational website does not provide remote access to that app. The owner is responsible for device access, credential protection and backups. No system can guarantee absolute security. This notice must be updated before introducing public accounts, new data collection, trackers or additional service providers.

Contact

The owner of Everyday Find Studio is responsible for privacy questions and requests. Email everydayfindstudioca@gmail.com to ask about information we hold, request access, correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We may ask for enough information to verify a request before responding.

For Pinterest's own practices, see its privacy policy. For Meta's practices, see its privacy policy.
